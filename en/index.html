Love is action!

Since 1978, New Life Mission - Aid has been working in Africa and Eastern Europe with projects in education, emergency relief, water initiatives, health, and the environment. Today, the important and extensive work continues, helping save lives and giving people new hope.

Together with local partners, we work strategically to manage resources in the best possible way. The projects today have a significant positive impact on individuals and local communities.

Aid and Emergency Relief

We lift people out of poverty by providing vital assistance, improving infrastructure, and offering safety and care.

Education

Education for children and young people provides opportunities, knowledge, and lasting value for individuals, families, and local communities.

Mission

Reaching people with the gospel is an important and natural part of all our projects. The Christian faith is the foundation of our work.

Community Development

Through active collaboration with local partners, we maintain a strong focus on the environment, such as cleaning up plastic waste in Kenya and creating safe and healthy workplaces.

Real Stories!

Real and moving stories from the people we help show that the work New Life carries out makes an important difference in the world for those who need it most.
