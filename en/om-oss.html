About us

New Life Mission - Aid was established in August 1978 and has since then worked purposefully with mission and humanitarian aid. Our commitment began in Kenya, where we built orphanages for children who were living under extremely difficult conditions in prisons together with their mothers. This was the beginning of an extensive international effort that today includes projects in Africa and Eastern Europe.

Over the years, we have sent thousands of tons of emergency aid to families, schools, and healthcare institutions in several Eastern European countries. In Kenya, we have established and rehabilitated orphanages, and founded preschools, primary schools, and a secondary school. In addition, we run projects for widows, social programs in slum areas, village development, and water projects with a focus on sustainability and self-help.

We work closely with local partners, authorities, and political stakeholders to ensure that our assistance creates lasting results and contributes to positive community development.

Vision

We seek to see people living in difficult circumstances get the opportunity to flourish – to grow spiritually and live with dignity, independence, and hope.

We want everyone to have access to life’s fundamental needs and the opportunity to build a sustainable future for themselves.

Mission

New Life Mission - Aid works where public support and systems are not sufficient.

We meet the needs in local communities directly, driven by Christ’s love and the belief that genuine love is shown through action.

Together with local partners, we provide humanitarian assistance and support spiritual growth. We emphasize sustainability, ownership, self-reliance, and the strengthening of local communities so that people can build better lives and become independent of external aid.
