Projects

Together with local partners, we work strategically to manage resources in the best possible way. The projects have a strong positive impact on people and local communities today.

Aid and Emergency Relief

We work actively to provide people with food, water, clothing, and a safe place to live. Slum areas and flood-affected regions of Kenya are examples of where we are making a difference.

Read more

Education

Education provides a dignified life and opportunities for individuals, families, and entire communities. We work closely with local partners and authorities to ensure that children and young people receive an education that gives them opportunities for work and a sustainable income.

Read more

Mission

Reaching people with the gospel is an important and natural part of all our projects. The Christian faith is the foundation of our work.

Read more

Community Development

Through several different projects, we aim to build a sustainable society that benefits both people and the environment – not only for the present generation, but for children, grandchildren, and the generations to come.
