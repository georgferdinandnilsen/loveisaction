Projects/Education

Education

Education provides a dignified life and opportunities for individuals, families, and entire communities. We work closely with local partners and authorities to ensure that children and young people receive an education that gives them opportunities for work and a sustainable income.

Education program for students

We believe that education provides knowledge and opportunities to break free from dependency and poverty. That is why we have established an education program for students, which also includes a mentorship program that helps them choose studies aligned with their abilities and talents.

As part of the program, we have also created a Service Club, where students, after completing their education, commit to giving back through mandatory trainee programs in our projects.

Primary school

We want to make primary education accessible to the most vulnerable children. That is why we support schooling for children from families who cannot afford to send them to school.

Through support and initiatives for establishing and operating primary schools in Maseno, Kitui, Kariobangi, Ayweyo, and Kavunzoni, we help transform the lives and futures for thousands of children in Kenya.
